Testing & training guide

CT Cardiac Calcium Score pilot

Prepared for Dr. Luis Ramos

This tool turns your calcium-score inputs into a complete, ready-to-paste report — the exact loop you showed us: per-vessel Agatston scores → total → MESA percentile → finished report text. It runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing.

What it does, and doesn't

It handles the pre-cognitive work: the math, the percentile, and the report boilerplate. It does not read from Exa or write into your report automatically (that is the next phase), and it does not interpret images. You are the radiologist.

How to use it

1
Enter the four vessel scores — Left Main, LAD, LCx, RCA. Or, if you have the aggregate, use the Total field.
2
Enter the patient factors — Age (45–84), Gender, Race/Ethnicity (White, African-American, Hispanic, Chinese).
3
Click Generate — you get the total, the MESA percentile, the probability of non-zero calcium, and the full report.
4
Click Copy — paste the report into your system.

Please run these tests

  • A normal case — a typical read from your day. Does the report match how you would write it?
  • Zero calcium (all vessels 0) — does the impression read correctly?
  • Total-only entry — enter just a total; confirm it does not invent vessel numbers.
  • Out-of-range age (try 40 or 90) — confirm it warns the MESA percentile is valid only 45–84.
  • Percentile check — compare its percentile to the official MESA tool for the same inputs. They should match.
  • The wording — read the boilerplate (TECHNIQUE, BACKGROUND). How close is it to your real template?
What we most want your eyes on: percentile accuracy vs. your MESA tool; whether the report is paste-ready or needs wording changes; your exact template language (we drafted from your screenshots — tell us the precise wording and we will match it); and anything missing or slow.

Known limits (this pilot, on purpose)

  • MESA percentile is valid for ages 45–84 and the four ethnicity categories — the MESA tool's own limits.
  • Boilerplate wording is our best draft from your screenshots; nailing your exact template text is a main goal of this test.
  • Standalone only — no Exa read/write yet. That is the next phase.

How to send feedback

Inside the tool there is a Feedback button. Note what worked, what didn't, any wording to change, and anything missing, then Copy it (or use the email button) and send it to Rick. Your notes go straight to Rick, and we turn them into the next version.

RadAssist runs locally and stores nothing. This guide and the tool contain no patient health information. Questions go to Rick, and Rick brings them to us.
